# Honest limits

The source package is build-ready, but the finished ISO cannot honestly be called 100% compatible with the two laptops until it is physically tested on both.

Exact pen, camera, fingerprint, touchscreen, and sensor subdevices can vary between units sold under the same laptop model. The kernel, firmware, and userspace support are included, but a device-specific quirk may still be needed after reading a hardware report.

The live account and settings reset after reboot unless the ISO is installed or a persistence partition is configured.
